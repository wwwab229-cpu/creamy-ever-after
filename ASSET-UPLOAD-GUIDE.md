# Creamy Ever After — Asset Upload Guide

Upload the approved original assets into these folders:

- `assets/logo/` — website logo files (prefer transparent PNG or SVG).
- `assets/images/hero/` — five real-work photos for the hero slideshow.
- `assets/images/gallery/` — ten real-work photos for the floating gallery.

## Suggested filenames

**Logo**
- `logo.png` (or `logo.svg`)

**Hero slideshow**
- `hero-01.jpg`
- `hero-02.jpg`
- `hero-03.jpg`
- `hero-04.jpg`
- `hero-05.jpg`

**Floating gallery**
- `gallery-01.jpg` through `gallery-10.jpg`

Keep the original image quality where practical. Use JPG or WebP for photos and transparent PNG/SVG for the logo. Do not rename or replace existing website files. After uploading, share the filenames or confirm the upload so the website can be connected to the assets.

These folders are upload locations only; the website design and layout are not changed by adding them.
